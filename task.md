# Daily AI Update Task

You are generating a daily AI digest. Search the web for each section below, then write a single markdown report to `reports/YYYY-MM-DD.md` (use today's actual date).

---

## What to collect

### 1. Trending GitHub Repositories
Search: `site:github.com trending AI machine learning today` and `github trending repositories artificial intelligence this week`
- Look for repos with strong recent star momentum (not just high total stars)
- Prioritize repos related to: LLMs, agents, RAG, diffusion, vision-language, AI infra/tooling
- Include: repo name, link, brief description, why it's interesting

### 2. Trending arXiv Papers
Search: `arxiv.org cs.AI cs.LG cs.CL new papers today` and check `https://huggingface.co/papers` for community-upvoted papers
- Focus on papers getting attention on social media / the HuggingFace community
- Prefer papers with novel methods, benchmarks, or surprising results
- Include: title, link to arxiv, 1-sentence summary of the key contribution

### 3. AI Blog Posts & Articles
Check recent posts from these sources (search for their latest):
- Hugging Face Blog (huggingface.co/blog)
- Google DeepMind Blog
- Towards Data Science (towardsdatascience.com)
- Sebastian Raschka's newsletter (magazine.sebastianraschka.com)
- The Batch by deeplearning.ai
- Last Week in AI (lastweekin.ai)
- Any other high-signal AI engineering posts getting shared widely

### 4. YouTube Videos
Search: `youtube AI engineering tutorial this week` and `youtube large language model new video`
- Check AI Engineer channel, Andrej Karpathy, Yannic Kilcher, 3Blue1Brown, Two Minute Papers
- Only include videos published in the last 7 days
- Include: channel name, video title, link, one line on what it covers
