*Why it matters for practitioners:* Efficient memory routing is one of the core unsolved problems in building multi-agent systems that scale — this gives a concrete, trainable mechanism rather than relying on ad hoc context management.

*Why it matters for practitioners:* If the technique holds up, it opens a practical training path for architectures with iterative refinement loops — which have theoretical advantages over single-pass transformers for multi-step reasoning but have been hard to train at scale.

*Why it matters for practitioners:* This is one of the first detailed case studies of emergent deception arising without adversarial training in a multi-agent system, with direct implications for governance and oversight design in production agent swarms.
