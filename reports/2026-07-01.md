## Trending Papers

Recent papers from arXiv (cs.AI / cs.LG / cs.CL) with strong community traction:

1. **SwarmAgentic: Towards Fully Automated Agentic System Generation via Swarm Intelligence**
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.15672
   Introduces a PSO-inspired framework that constructs and jointly optimises multi-agent systems from scratch through language-driven exploration, evaluated on six open-ended real-world tasks.

2. **SoK: Agentic Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG): Taxonomy, Architectures, Evaluation, and Research Directions**
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.07379
   First unified framework formalising Agentic RAG as a finite-horizon POMDP, exposing gaps in fragmented architectures and calling out unresolved reliability risks.

3. **LiteResearcher: A Scalable Agentic RL Training Framework for Deep Research Agent**
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.17931
   Decouples synthetic data generation from real-world search during RL training to stabilise and scale deep research agents — LiteResearcher-4B achieves 71.3% on GAIA and 78.0% on Xbench (open-source SOTA).

4. **Perception-Aware Policy Optimization for Multimodal Reasoning**
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.06448
   Proposes a policy optimisation method that aligns the model's visual perception with its reasoning chain, reducing hallucinations in multimodal QA.

5. **VisualFLIP: Do Predictions Depend on Task-Critical Visual Evidence in Multimodal Reasoning?**
   https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.07872
   Diagnostic benchmark that flips task-critical visual regions to test whether VLMs actually ground their answers in the right visual evidence — many popular models fail.

6. **SoK: The Attack Surface of Agentic AI — Tools, and Autonomy**
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22928
   Systematic security analysis of agentic AI systems, cataloguing novel attack vectors introduced by tool use, persistent memory, and autonomous action chaining.

7. **Self-Evolving Multi-Agent Systems via Textual Backpropagation**
   https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.09046
   Multi-agent systems that improve their own inter-agent communication protocols using gradient-free textual backpropagation — a step toward self-improving agent teams.

8. **Agentic Reasoning for Large Language Models**
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.12538
   Comprehensive survey structuring the field into foundational agentic reasoning, self-evolving agents, and collective multi-agent reasoning — a useful map of where the field stands in 2026.
