## Trending Papers

**CentaurBench: Benchmarking LLM Capabilities on Augmenting vs. Automating Real-World Work Tasks** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18554
Finds that automation ranking and augmentation ranking are only modestly correlated, and the top automation model loses augmentation quality on 5 of 7 tasks—on 3 tasks, the unaided worker beats every assisted condition.
*Why it matters for practitioners:* If you're choosing a model for human-in-the-loop augmentation (the majority of enterprise AI deployments), relying on automation-focused leaderboard rankings will systematically mislead you toward the wrong model.

**Let's Scale Step by Step: Compute-Efficient Hyperparameter Transfer for Large-Scale Mixture-of-Experts** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20061
Proposes a two-step framework for MoE training—derive optimal learning rates via μP adapted for MoE + Multi-head Latent Attention + Muon, then extrapolate to trillion-token horizons via predictive scaling laws—validated on a 155B-total/17B-active model.
*Why it matters for practitioners:* Hyperparameter tuning at MoE scale is expensive enough to break training budgets; this gives a principled, compute-efficient transfer path that should meaningfully reduce ablation runs.

**Chained Recursive Language Models for Multi-Iteration Reasoning** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05124
Proposes an inference-time architecture where the same model is invoked repeatedly as fresh reasoning roots—each receiving the original problem without inheriting conversational history—to prevent early-step error accumulation in multi-hop tasks.
*Why it matters for practitioners:* For extraction, counting, ordering, or multi-hop reasoning chains, the chained-restart pattern is a drop-in inference change that requires no fine-tuning, making it immediately applicable to agent pipelines.

**Understanding Evolution Strategies for LLM Reasoning: Broader Reasoning Coverage than GRPO** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27351
Shows that evolution-strategy-based training produces broader coverage of reasoning trajectories than GRPO under matched compute budgets, offering the first systematic comparison of the two paradigms in the post-GRPO era.
*Why it matters for practitioners:* GRPO became the default post-training recipe for reasoning in 2025; this is the first principled challenge to that default and gives teams a concrete alternative to evaluate when reasoning diversity matters more than peak-path performance.

**Stealing Reasoning Traces from Proprietary LLM APIs** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09867
Demonstrates that hidden chain-of-thought reasoning (as used by o-series and Gemini Thinking models) can be reconstructed from observable API signals—token probabilities, latency, output distributions—without direct model access.
*Why it matters for practitioners:* If you or your competitors rely on reasoning trace concealment as a product differentiator, this paper significantly narrows how defensible that differentiation is under adversarial probing.
