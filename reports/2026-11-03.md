## Trending Papers

**Strategist: Evolving Reasoning with Agents** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03618
A NeurIPS 2026 submission presenting a meta-agent that automatically writes and refines inference strategies (chains of thought, tool-use policies) for downstream task-solving agents.
*Why it matters for practitioners:* Externalizes "how to reason" into an evolvable artifact separate from model weights — a design pattern that lets you improve agent behavior at inference time without retraining.

**MultiAgent-OPD: End-to-End Training of Hierarchical Multi-Agent Systems with On-Policy Distillation** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23234
Trains a Leader, Searcher, and Answerer team end-to-end using on-policy distillation, with a frozen teacher providing step-level token feedback across role boundaries.
*Why it matters for practitioners:* Directly addresses the credit-assignment problem in multi-role agent training — on-policy distillation lets you propagate learning signals through an entire hierarchical agent stack without reward hacking at handoffs.

**EAGR: Evidence-Guided Adaptive Graph Retrieval** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01910
A multi-hop RAG method where an LLM judges whether gathered evidence is sufficient before issuing another retrieval hop, using graph-structured document relationships.
*Why it matters for practitioners:* Adaptive stopping reduces unnecessary retrieval hops and hallucination in complex QA pipelines — achieves the highest LLM-based answer accuracy on HotPotQA and MuSiQue in reported evals.

**Multi-Teacher Reasoning Distillation for LLM Recommendation with Self-Exploration** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02457
Trains a student LLM using several teacher models, selecting which teacher's reasoning trace to learn from based on a utility measure rather than treating all traces equally.
*Why it matters for practitioners:* Utility-based trace selection is a transferable technique for any distillation pipeline where teacher quality is uneven — addresses the often-ignored problem of noisy teacher supervision.

**Early Stopping in Large Reasoning Models** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00166
An empirical survey and audit of efficiency and reliability trade-offs in large reasoning models, focusing on when to terminate extended chain-of-thought generation.
*Why it matters for practitioners:* Quantifies the compute-vs-quality curve of extended reasoning at inference time — directly actionable for teams budgeting o1/r1-style model calls in production.
