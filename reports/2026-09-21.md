## Trending Papers

**Atria Dawn: The Dawn of Agentic Superintelligence** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15818
Shanghai AI Lab's 744B MoE foundation model trained end-to-end via a Verifiable Experience Pipeline connecting tool interactions to executable environments with externally-verified outcomes; includes a case study of 769 tasks across 56 participants analyzing how humans and agents actually divided labor during development.
*Why it matters for practitioners:* The human-AI collaboration analysis is the rare empirical look at what "human oversight" means at the project level — agents proposed methods and implemented revisions while humans retained final decisions and guided exploration — giving teams a concrete model for dividing work rather than defaulting to all-or-nothing delegation.

**GVPO++: Group Variance Policy Optimization for LLM Post-Training and On-Policy Distillation** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21432
Extended version of GVPO (NeurIPS 2025) that replaces GRPO's importance-sampling instability with a gradient scheme proven to recover the exact KL-constrained reward-maximization solution, and generalizes to a broad family of on-policy distillation objectives.
*Why it matters for practitioners:* Training instability in GRPO has been a persistent pain point for teams doing RL fine-tuning; GVPO++ offers a drop-in replacement with a theoretical guarantee on uniqueness of the optimal solution and no importance-sampling sensitivity.

**DENSE: Distilling Agent Trajectories into Evidence-Grounded Shortcut Trees for Self-Refinement** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21423
Framework that distills past agent execution traces into reusable evidence-grounded shortcut trees, enabling self-refinement without requiring post-hoc outcome labels; achieves 7–15 pp strict pass-rate improvements on Terminal-Bench 2.1 while using 19–44% fewer tokens on reruns.
*Why it matters for practitioners:* Self-improvement without labeled outcomes is the key unlock for continuous agent improvement in production, where manually labeling trajectories at scale is impractical.

**FreeToken: Efficient Edge-Native MoE Serving with Bandwidth-Adaptive Execution** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16157
UC Berkeley / MIT system that co-designs model layout, expert residency, CPU-GPU execution, and KV-cache reuse to run 35B MoE models at 39 tokens/s on 8 GB VRAM, and 753B models on a single consumer GPU.
*Why it matters for practitioners:* FreeToken rewrites the hardware assumptions for local model deployment — teams that ruled out frontier-scale open-weight models for on-device or air-gapped use cases should revisit that decision.

**AgentDistill: Training-Free Agent Distillation with Generalizable MCP Boxes** — https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.14728
Method for distilling the tool-use capabilities of large teacher agents into smaller student agents without any gradient updates, using MCP (Model Context Protocol) tool boxes as the transfer medium.
*Why it matters for practitioners:* Training-free capability transfer is practically important for teams that can't afford to fine-tune — MCP boxes as the distillation unit is a clever alignment with where the industry's tooling is standardizing anyway.
