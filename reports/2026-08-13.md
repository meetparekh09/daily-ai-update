## Trending Papers

**Fisher-R1: Training LLM Agents for Reliable Hypothesis Testing** — [arxiv.org/abs/2608.07437](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07437)
Introduces P-Bench (425 realistic hypothesis-testing tasks across economics, biology, and medicine) and Fisher-R1-14B, an RL-trained agent that outperforms GPT-5.4 and DeepSeekV4-Pro by 21% average relative improvement in single-trial success (submitted August 7, 2026).
*Why it matters for practitioners:* Demonstrates that a targeted RL training regime on a well-designed benchmark can produce a 14B model that beats much larger proprietary models at a structured analytical task — a concrete template for specialist agent training.

**SIRI: Self-Internalizing Reinforcement Learning with Intrinsic Skills for LLM Agent Training** — [arxiv.org/abs/2606.02355](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02355)
A training framework where agents discover and internalize reusable skill abstractions through intrinsic RL rewards, rather than requiring a hand-specified skill library designed by engineers.
*Why it matters for practitioners:* Eliminates the brittle manual step of defining what skills your agent should have — the model learns which decompositions are useful from its own training experience, lowering the cost of capable agent development.

**The Interplay of Harness Design and Post-Training in LLM Agents** — [arxiv.org/abs/2606.25447](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25447)
Empirical analysis showing that the scaffolding and execution harness surrounding an LLM agent significantly affects whether post-training techniques (RLHF, fine-tuning) transfer to agentic behavior — certain harness choices actively interfere with alignment.
*Why it matters for practitioners:* If you've been applying post-training independently of your harness design, this paper explains why results are inconsistent — harness and training are co-dependent decisions that must be optimized together.

**Retrospective Progress-Aware Self-Refinement (RePro) for LLM Agent Training** — [arxiv.org/abs/2606.14302](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.14302)
A training framework that has agents self-generate progress signals via a forward-then-reflect rollout paradigm, using Retrospection Warmup to bootstrap the progress model before full RL training.
*Why it matters for practitioners:* Sidesteps the sparse-reward problem in long-horizon agent tasks by training the agent to produce its own dense feedback signal — practical progress for training on multi-step workflows without extensive human annotation.

**Toward Auditable AI Scientists: A Hypothesis Evolution Protocol for LLM Agents** — [arxiv.org/abs/2607.09195](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09195)
A structured protocol for tracking and auditing how LLM agents develop, revise, and discard scientific hypotheses over multi-turn research sessions, enabling verification of the reasoning chain behind agent-generated conclusions.
*Why it matters for practitioners:* As agents are deployed for research and data analysis tasks, auditability of hypothesis generation becomes a compliance and trust requirement — this provides a concrete standard for building that audit trail into your system.
