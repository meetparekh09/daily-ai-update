## Trending Papers

**AgentRedBench: Dynamic Redteaming and Integration-Aware Defense for LLM Agents over SaaS Integrations** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02240
215 indirect prompt injection scenarios across 24 enterprise SaaS integrations (Gmail, Salesforce, Jira, and others), plus a deployable guard model (AGENTREDGUARD) that cuts attack success rate from 69.9% to 2.4% with near-zero false positives on benign traffic.
*Why it matters for practitioners:* The benchmark covers the actual production attack surface — tool-response content from third-party integrations the user doesn't write or control — and ships an open-source guard trained on that distribution, not chat-style data.

**TCOD: Exploring Temporal Curriculum in On-Policy Distillation for Multi-Turn Autonomous Agents** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.24005
A temporal curriculum framework for on-policy distillation that starts from the easiest sub-goals and progressively increases difficulty, reducing average action steps by ~3 while improving agent success rates by up to 15.7%; the TCOD-F2B variant requires no pre-collected demonstrations.
*Why it matters for practitioners:* TCOD-F2B is a drop-in improvement over standard on-policy distillation for multi-turn agents — no extra data collection overhead, directly applicable to existing training pipelines.

**DemoPSD: Disagreement-Modulated Policy Self-Distillation** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02502
A self-distillation approach that detects where student and teacher policies disagree and attenuates supervision at precisely those tokens to avoid forcing the student to imitate behaviors that depend on privileged teacher context.
*Why it matters for practitioners:* Solves a core training pathology — teacher coercion on tokens where the student has no access to privileged information — improving stability and sample efficiency for imitation-based agent training.

**Recursive Self-Improvement in AI: From Bounded Self-Refinement to Autonomous Research Loops** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07663
A survey mapping the trajectory from constrained self-refinement to fully autonomous AI research loops, examining what enabling conditions and failure modes characterize each regime on the path toward systems like autoresearch.
*Why it matters for practitioners:* Provides a principled framework for understanding where current autonomous experimentation systems sit on the capability curve and what safety properties are needed before trusting them unsupervised.

**Osprey: Production-Ready Agentic AI for Safety-Critical Control Systems** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.15066
A plan-first agentic framework deployed at a real particle accelerator facility that generates complete execution plans with all dependencies for human review before any hardware is touched, plus a dynamic tool classifier that selects only necessary tools per task.
*Why it matters for practitioners:* Demonstrates that plan-before-execute with mandatory human review is achievable without sacrificing agent autonomy; the architecture is directly portable to any high-stakes deployment where irreversible actions require approval.
