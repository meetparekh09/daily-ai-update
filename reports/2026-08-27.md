*Why it matters for practitioners:* It directly addresses the "human-in-the-loop before irreversible action" architectural gap for agents operating in the physical world, with design patterns for pre-execution review and tool selection that generalize far beyond safety-critical domains.

A fine-tuning method that trains long-horizon LLM agents with significantly reduced GPU requirements by optimizing for executable state outcomes rather than token-level supervision.
*Why it matters for practitioners:* Bringing long-horizon agent fine-tuning within reach of teams without multi-GPU clusters changes the economics of agent specialization — a practitioner with a single A100 can now train domain-specific long-horizon agents.
