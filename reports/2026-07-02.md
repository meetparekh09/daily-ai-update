Introduces a benchmark that evaluates AI agents on realistic, multi-step scientific research workflows involving text, images, and structured data — filling a gap where existing benchmarks tested single-turn capability rather than sustained agentic reasoning.

### 4. [MedCTA: A Benchmark for Clinical Tool Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11702)
A domain-specific evaluation suite for AI agents that use clinical tools (lab queries, EHR lookups, imaging APIs) — notable because it surfaces failure modes that generic agent benchmarks miss when the stakes are medical.

### 5. [RAFT: Data Refinement and Adaptive Distillation for Domain Fine-Tuning with Alleviated Forgetting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.RAFT)
Proposes an efficient recipe for adapting a general-purpose LLM to a domain without catastrophic forgetting of pre-trained knowledge — particularly relevant for enterprise teams wanting to fine-tune on proprietary data while retaining broad capability.
