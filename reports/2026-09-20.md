## Trending Papers

**τ^τ-Bench: An Environment for End-To-End, Realistic Agent Construction** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04611
A benchmark where the task is building a production agent (given real business records, a simulated client, and an inherited codebase), with Claude Opus 5 under Claude Code passing only 23.9% of simulations vs. an expert-authored ceiling of 82.2%.
*Why it matters for practitioners:* The 58-point gap between best available tooling and expert-built agents reveals that current agent frameworks, not just model capability, are the limiting factor in real deployments.

**Score Centering Stabilizes Off-Policy Reinforcement Learning** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20807
A simple advantage-estimation technique that mitigates the training-inference distribution mismatch that destabilizes GRPO/REINFORCE-style RL training of LLMs, without the compute cost of eliminating mismatch entirely.
*Why it matters for practitioners:* Off-policy instability is the main reason RL fine-tuning runs go off the rails; score centering is a low-cost fix that can be applied to existing RL pipelines without architectural changes.

**Learning What to Retain: Gated-Memory Routing for Efficient Collaboration in Multi-Agent LLM Systems** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00237
A learned gating mechanism that selectively routes information into shared memory in multi-agent LLM systems, reducing redundant context propagation while preserving coordination fidelity (EMNLP 2026 Main).
*Why it matters for practitioners:* Memory bloat in long-running multi-agent pipelines is a real cost and latency problem; gated routing gives teams a principled way to control what agents share rather than broadcasting everything.

**PipeSwift: Revisiting Pipeline Parallelism for Large-Scale Completion-Oriented Agentic LLM Serving** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16491
Redesigns pipeline parallelism for agentic workloads where total completion time—not token throughput—is the governing metric, making traditional pipeline scheduling for chatbots suboptimal for agents.
*Why it matters for practitioners:* Teams serving agentic workloads who tuned their infrastructure for chatbot throughput are likely leaving significant latency on the table; this reframes the optimization target.

**RAG-Safety-Bench: Reliable Evaluation of Retrieval-Augmented LLM Safety** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11758
Tests safety across five open-source LLMs under four RAG conditions, finding that baseline safety guardrails do not carry through to RAG settings and that even benign retrieved documents can induce unsafe generation (EMNLP 2026).
*Why it matters for practitioners:* Production RAG systems are often deployed under the assumption that model-level safety training transfers — this benchmark shows that assumption is wrong and quantifies the gap.
