## Trending Papers

- **DanceOPD: On-Policy Generative Field Distillation** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27377
  Introduces an on-policy distillation framework for flow-matching generative models that routes each sample to a single capability field and trains via a simple velocity-MSE objective, simplifying multi-teacher distillation.

- **OPID: On-Policy Skill Distillation for Agentic Reinforcement Learning** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26790
  Proposes extracting hierarchical (episode- and step-level) "skills" from an agent's own on-policy trajectories and injecting them back as self-distillation signal, improving sample efficiency on ALFWorld, WebShop, and search-based QA.

- **World Models: A Comprehensive Survey of Architectures, Methodologies, Reasoning Paradigms, and Applications** — https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.00133
  A 4-axis taxonomy (architecture, methodology, reasoning strategy, application) unifying state-space, transformer, diffusion, and physics-informed approaches to world modeling, accepted to IEEE Access.

- **In-Context World Modeling for Robotic Control** — (arXiv, June 2026; see Hugging Face Daily Papers trending list, https://huggingface.co/papers/trending)
  Explores using in-context learning to let a single world model adapt to new robotic control tasks without weight updates — one of this week's top-trending Hugging Face papers.

- **A Pre-Training Analogue of Grokking in Language Models: Tracing Delayed Grammatical Generalization** — https://arxiv.org/list/cs.LG/current
  Shows that grokking-style delayed generalization (previously observed mainly in small algorithmic tasks) also appears during LLM pre-training on grammatical structure, with generalization lagging memorization by many steps.
